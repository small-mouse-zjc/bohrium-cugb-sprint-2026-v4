Gas hydrate (GH) may form in marine sediment at site CUG-3 [3], and we used 25 samples to test this idea. The dissociation pressure of GH may increase by 0.9 MPa when salinity rises from 0 to 3.5 wt% at 4 °C [4]. We did not observe any dissociation above 12 MPa, and the permeability was 2 times higher than that of the clay. The dissociation pressure is sensitive to salinity.
