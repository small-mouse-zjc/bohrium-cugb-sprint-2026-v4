# 结构化笔记

## Abstract

- [problem] We investigate how CO2 injection changes the phase behaviour of gas hydrate-bearing marine sediment.
  - anchor: `§1 ¶1 / p.1 [9-109]`
- [data] Core samples from 3 sites in the Qionghai area were tested at 12 MPa and 4 °C.
  - anchor: `§1 ¶1 / p.1 [110-188]`
- [finding] Results show that CO2 displaces methane hydrate and forms a mixed hydrate phase within 6 hours.
  - anchor: `§1 ¶1 / p.1 [189-284]`

## Introduction

- [problem] The research question is whether CO2 can be stored in hydrate reservoirs without destabilising the overlying sediment.
  - anchor: `§2 ¶1 / p.1 [299-417]`
- [limitation] Previous work examined pure methane hydrate only, and the effect of CO2 on phase equilibrium remains unclear.
  - anchor: `§2 ¶1 / p.1 [418-527]`
- [problem] Here we examine the coupled effect of CO2 fraction and salinity.
  - anchor: `§2 ¶1 / p.1 [528-592]`

## Materials and Methods

- [data] Samples of 25 g wet sediment were collected from 3 gravity cores.
  - anchor: `§3 ¶1 / p.2 [616-681]`
- [method] Experiments were performed in a high-pressure cell at 12 MPa and 4 °C, using a CO2 fraction of 0.3 to 0.9.
  - anchor: `§3 ¶1 / p.2 [682-788]`
- [premise] We assume a constant pore pressure of 12 MPa during each run.
  - anchor: `§3 ¶1 / p.2 [829-890]`

## Results

- [finding] CO2 injection increased the hydrate dissociation temperature by 2.4 °C at a CO2 fraction of 0.7.
  - anchor: `§4 ¶1 / p.3 [900-996]`
- [background] The measured permeability dropped from 120 mD to 45 mD after 6 hours.
  - anchor: `§4 ¶1 / p.3 [997-1066]`
- [finding] Results indicate a mixed hydrate phase at CO2 fractions above 0.5.
  - anchor: `§4 ¶1 / p.3 [1067-1133]`

## Discussion

- [finding] The observed shift is consistent with a mixed hydrate stability field.
  - anchor: `§5 ¶1 / p.4 [1146-1216]`
- [finding] Our data suggest that CO2 storage capacity is limited at salinities above 3.5 wt%.
  - anchor: `§5 ¶1 / p.4 [1217-1299]`
- [finding] The comparison with pure methane runs shows a lower dissociation pressure at 0.7 CO2 fraction.
  - anchor: `§5 ¶1 / p.4 [1300-1394]`

## Limitations

- [limitation] A limitation of this study is that only 3 cores were tested, so spatial variability cannot be assessed.
  - anchor: `§6 ¶1 / p.4 [1408-1511]`
- [limitation] The model does not account for sediment compaction, and the temperature control remains uncertain at 4 °C.
  - anchor: `§6 ¶1 / p.4 [1512-1618]`
- [data] Further work should test natural cores.
  - anchor: `§6 ¶1 / p.4 [1619-1658]`
